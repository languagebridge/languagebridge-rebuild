# Pricing & Packaging

**How LanguageBridge is priced, and why every number is what it is.** Priced to be an easy "yes" out of existing EL budgets while sustaining healthy margin. **This is confirmed, approved list pricing (September 2026).** Figures marked *(estimate)* are external benchmarks or planning inputs — not the LanguageBridge prices, which are the tier tables below.

**Owner:** Justin Bernard · Last updated: September 2026

---

## The pricing principle

**We charge per *English-learner* student per year — not per total enrollment.** Only EL students use the tool, so a 5,000-student district with 600 EL students pays for 600. This is honest, aligns price with cost, and makes LanguageBridge dramatically cheaper than tools that bill on whole-district headcount.

Everything is annual, and every paid tier includes onboarding, teacher training, email support, and the LEA analytics dashboard.

## Tiers

### Free Pilot — land and prove it
- **Price:** $0 for one semester
- **Scope:** up to 150 EL students, one building
- **Includes:** full product, onboarding, a signed DPA, and an end-of-pilot usage report
- **Why it exists:** EL directors buy on evidence. A free semester turns a demo into a district reference (this is how the Parma MVP began).

### Per-EL-student annual license (volume-tiered)

| Tier | EL students | Price / EL student / year |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | 1 – 500 | **$12** |
| **Growth** | 501 – 2,000 | **$9** |
| **District** | 2,001 – 10,000 | **$6** |
| **Enterprise** | 10,000+ | **$4** *(custom)* |

Prices are marginal per tier at the district/enterprise level (a district crossing a threshold blends rates), or simple flat-per-student at Starter/Growth — whichever the customer prefers.

### Building / site license — for simple procurement
- **$3,000 per building / year**, covering up to 400 EL students in that building.
- For schools that would rather buy one predictable line item than count students. Above 400 EL students in a building, the per-student tiers are cheaper — we'll quote whichever favors the school.

### District license — custom
- Custom annual price built from the per-student tiers with a district-wide discount, **floor $10,000/year**.
- Adds a named account manager, a live professional-development day, roster/rollout assistance, and priority support.

## Administrative & optional fees

Standard onboarding, self-serve teacher training, email support, and the analytics dashboard are **included** in every paid tier — never a surprise line item. Optional, clearly-priced add-ons:

| Add-on | Fee *(estimate)* |

## Why this is defensible (the short version)

- **It clears cost with room to spare.** Our infrastructure runs ~$2–5 per active student per year (see [COST-MODEL-AND-UNIT-ECONOMICS.md](COST-MODEL-AND-UNIT-ECONOMICS.md)). At $6–12, gross margin is ~65–85% — before proprietary TTS drives cost down further at scale.
- **It's fundable.** Per-EL-student pricing maps directly to **Title III** (the federal EL funding stream) and newcomer/immigrant-education grants, so it comes from money schools already hold for exactly this purpose.
- **It rewards scale on both sides.** Volume tiers give districts a real discount while our per-student cost also falls with scale — the economics improve together, which is what lets us reach thousands of schools.

> **Pricing confirmed by LanguageBridge leadership (September 2026).** The tiers above are approved list pricing — justifiable line-by-line to a school business office and profitable at the unit level. Adjust only by deliberate decision, not per deal.
