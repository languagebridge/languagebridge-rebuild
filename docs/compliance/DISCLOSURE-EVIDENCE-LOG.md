## 1. Why this log exists

Ohio Revised Code 2921.42 (unlawful interest in a public contract) and ORC 102.03 (state ethics law) turn substantially on whether a public employee's outside business interest was disclosed and whether the employee had authority over, or benefited from, a public-funds contract. Separately, Parma City School District's internal policies on outside employment, IP, and conflicts of interest depend on whether written disclosure was made through recognized channels.

The evidence inventoried here establishes that:

1. LanguageBridge LLC, and Justin Bernard's role as its founder/builder, were disclosed through **multiple independent official channels** (administrative approval, curriculum/DIS review, IT infrastructure, superintendent-level communication, and a signed district contract).
2. Disclosure was **bilateral and acknowledged** (responses from Principal, Superintendent, CFO/Treasurer, EL Coordinator, Technical Systems Manager, and IT staff).
3. The pilot operated at **$0 cost** to Parma — no public-contract dollars changed hands during the disclosed period.
4. Justin held **no approval authority** over the procurement decision for the pilot.

Each entry below should remain preservable without any Parma-district account access. Preservation status is tracked per entry.

Entries are ordered chronologically.
